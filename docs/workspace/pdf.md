# PDF

Keep the page in view, and leave a few useful marks for later.

## Begin reading

1. Open a PDF from Home or Recently opened.
2. Use the page field, thumbnails or outline to move through it.
3. Open the sidebar to find text, highlights or comments.

New PDFs start with the whole page fitted to the window. Use Fit to return to that view, or choose Fit width from the zoom menu.

Use the single-rectangle or split-rectangle button to switch between one and two pages. New reading sessions start with one page.

Pages shows larger previews with their numbers underneath. The outlined page is your current position.

With the PDF focused, Left and Right move between pages. Up and Down keep normal continuous scrolling. Arrow keys inside an editable field keep their usual editing behaviour.

## Mark what matters

Choose a highlight, note or drawing tool. Save writes changes to the open file. Undo lets you revisit an edit.

Use the page overview to reorder, rotate or add pages. Export comments from the Comments heading when you want your notes as Markdown or JSON.

## Copy an image or table

Drag an embedded image into Notes or another image-aware app. You can also right-click and choose **Copy Image**.

Select a table or right-click inside it, then choose **Copy Table**. Paste into Numbers, Excel or another spreadsheet. A small progress message appears while copying, with Cancel available.

Table recognition runs on this Mac and requires macOS 26 or later. Check numbers and merged cells after pasting. Vector-only diagrams may not offer Copy Image.

## Keep the original in mind

Editing can affect digital signatures. Signed or restricted PDFs show an editing lock. Review the warning before allowing changes, or save a separate copy.

## Add a PDF and keep reading

Double-click another PDF in Finder, use the plus beside the last bottom tab, or drop a PDF onto the document. It opens in the same bottom tab group. Opening an already open PDF selects its existing tab.

Drag a tab left or right to change the order. The insertion line shows where it will go. Drop it on a tab in another remile window to move it there, or onto the desktop to open a separate window. Press Escape to cancel.

Selecting a tab keeps its document, reading position and pending edits. Close it when finished. Closing a changed document offers Save, Cancel and Don’t Save.

Reopening a PDF restores its last page and position. Search results open at the matching passage. Reading positions are stored separately, without changing the PDF.
